About the figures. Built from NHS England's monthly referral to treatment (RTT) data, counting people still waiting to start treatment ("incomplete pathways") at England's acute NHS trusts. Trusts are matched on their NHS organisation code; trusts that merge are combined so their figures stay comparable. Trusts that did not submit data are listed at the top of the page. The median wait is estimated from NHS England's weekly waiting bands. Long waits count people waiting more than 52, 65 and 78 weeks. Month-on-month changes compare only the trusts that reported in both months, and changes smaller than 0.3 percentage points (or 0.5% for counts) are shown as "no real change". The current milestone is 70% of people waiting under 18 weeks by March 2027, with each trust expected to reach at least 65%; the constitutional standard of 92% is due by March 2029.

Disclaimer: this is an unofficial reporting tool and is not linked to any NHS bodies or entities. It is purely for research purposes, using monthly data made publicly available by NHS England.
